KNICKERBOCKER (Copyright. 1936. by International News Service) TOLEDO, Spain, Oct. 30.--The Spanish civil war entered its de cisive phase today as the white armies encircling Madrid complete ly, save for a small gap in the west and southwest, prepared for a final assault. Its purpose is to capture the capital before reinforcements for the reds from the Soviet Union ar rive. The true importance of this war for the outside world became ap parent today. It is whether an in- j temational conflict can be avoided. Surprise Attack Feared Already a report is current here that Madrid has received over 100 Russian planes with pilots, and that they are being saved for a great and overpowering surprise attack. Toledo, Seville and Caceres were visited today by Madrid bombers which observers here say were of Russian types. They did little damage, but a persistent report on this side has it that another group of Madrid bombers attacking Tala vera used gas. If this is true, it means a new period of horrors is in store for this war-harried country. The whites for the last six weeks had undisputed mastery of the air, but if it is a fact Madrid has ob tained 100 new planes it will mean the slaughter will be intensified many fold. Insurgent Planes Busy Far from being inactive, white planes, spurred on by the belief Russia intends landing shiploads of arms at red ports, have begun to bomb them. On the first night of an expedition over Car tagena they dropped over 14 tons of bombs from nine planes which alternately and uninterruptedly visited the red harbor from 9:30 p. m. until 1 a. m. Among the bombs dropped were 36 of a quarter-ton each. The whites reported they believed they destroyed all the docks and much shipping, which was declared most ly Russian. Meanwhile, the ,white effort is to reach Madrid as soon as pos sible in order to obtain promised German and Italian recognition, which would mean they could have unlimited supplies. At the same time the Soviet Union's continued recognition of the present Madrid government after it moved to Catalonia would bring almost unavoidable conflict with the Fascist powers. --VOTE AMERICAN-- Strike Ends, Ship Sails BOSTON, Oct. 30 (I.N.S.)The liner Susan B.
