BOY DROPS 25 FEET TO DEATH IN HIS COASTER While riding in his coaster wag on, Richard Clouse, 5 years old, 1318 North Thirty-seventh st., plunged 25 feet to his death late yesterday. He was killed when his head struck a rail of the Milwaukee road tracks near his home. There is no guard rail at the edge of the sheer drop to the tracks below. Irving Clouse, his father, is an employe of the Milwaukee road. His mother and a sister, Mary Ann, also survive. Funeral requiem will be read at 9 a. m. Friday in Holy Rosary church. Burial will be in Holy Cross cemetery. NATIONAL BODY IS ABOLISHED BY SOCIALISTS The convention voted to abolish the national committee and replace it with a national executive com mittee which will guide the party in the intervals between national conventions. The decision was made by a large majority after Judge Jacob Panken, New York, explained: "The party's committee on the constitution, of which I am chair man, decided not to continue in ex istence a body which did not func tion." Under the amendment to article four, the membership of the nation al executive committee will include the national chairman and 10 mem bers. The old national committee only had eight members and a chairman. With spectacular and principal business completed, the delegates showed a disposition not to waste time on petty details, restricted the time of speeches. Considerable debate is expected when the delegates reach proposed constitutional changes which are intended to make the party machin ery smoother running, better adapt ed to the needs of a rapidly grow ing movement and more representa tive of the distant parts of the country.
