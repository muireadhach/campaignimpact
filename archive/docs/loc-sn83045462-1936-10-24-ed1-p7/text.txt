I stated that Maryland Is a good index to the country because in the last 12 presidential elections the result In Maryland coincided with the result in the country as a whole. This statement needs qualification with respect to two elections. In 1904 and 1908 Maryland gave a popular major ity to the Republicans, as did the rest of the country. I have regretfully cut these arias with a deep felt apology to the masters who com posed them, but with the firm convic tion that this was the only way they might be presented most effectively over the microphone. Last year Slip Madigan was the leader in a national poll of coach popularity, getting 349,444 votes. St. Mary's won students, endowments and a beautiful new home. Whenever one of his lads messed up a play, fumbled the ball or missed an easy catch, Madigan would tell them how Tom Deegan would liaive handled that play. The name might have been Dugan, but, at any rate, it was some such Irish name. Deegan, it seemed, had been one of Madigan's mates at Notre Dame. When a player put over something fast or clever, Madigan would recall that Tom Deegan won such and such a close game by just such a play. The boys got to asking themselves, when they went to bed at night, whether they had played that day as Tom Deegan would play. He was their mentor, even in training discipline and be havior. When St. Mary's and Tom Deegan had been winning games for years, somebody wrote a book about the late Knute Rockne. It listed every man on all Rockne s squads, but no Tom Deegan. The omission was called to Madi gan's attention.
