Aged Actress Dies in Poverty NEW YORK--In a little three room flat she shared with another aged actress, Sarah Bird, Carrie Colburn, 73 years old, who played with Trixie Friganza in her hey day, died yesterday. Miss Colburn's stage career in cluded important roles in Over the Hill and Barrie's Little Minister. She will be buried on Wednesday by the Actors' Fund of America.
