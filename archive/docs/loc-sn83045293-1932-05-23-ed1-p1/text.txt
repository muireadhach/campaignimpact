PARTYPASSES FIV E BILLION BELIEF HAM A plank demanding a federal ap propriation of $5,000,000,000 for im mediate relief for those in need to supplement state and local appro priations was passed today, as the Socialist national convention con tinued its sessions for drafting a platform today. Considerable discussion on an other plank providing for a federal appropriation of $5,000,000,000 for public work to relieve unemploy ment developed. The delegates, while in favor of the proposals for the appropriation, insisted upon more specifically stat ing in what manner this money is to be used. Ask Idle Factories For Workers. An amendment by Darlington Hoopes, Pennsylvania, to provide for legislation for turning over idle land, factories and equipment to the unemployed workers to allow them to produce food, fuel and clothing, was adopted by a motion of 78 to 62. Sheriff Al Benson in a speech sup ported the proposal and the Wis consin delegation voted for it. Another amendment offered by John M. Glenn, Washington, pro posing that the government encour age and aid the establishment of industrial exchanges and the tak ing over of factories for the unem ployed was defeated. The plank as finally adopted reads: Plank as Finally Adopted. "A federal appropriation of $5,- 000,000,000 for public work and roads, reforestration, slub clear ance and decent homes for workers by the federal government, states and cities an dproviding for legisla tion for turning over idle land, fac tories and equipment to unem ployed workers to allow them to produce food, fuel and clothing." Other planks adopted are for a six-hour day and five-day week, without reduction in wages and the comprehensive and sufficient of three public employment agencies. The convention this afternoon continued its discussion on th ques tion of a compulsory system of un employment compensation or insur ance with a division among the dele gates as to whether insurance or compensation should be favored and as to whether such compensation should be based on contributions by employers or raised by federal tax ation on incomes gifts and inheri tances.
